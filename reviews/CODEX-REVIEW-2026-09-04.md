## FINDINGS

1. **(a) arithmetic error — the AP63205 efficiency is defensible; its input-power calculation is not.** The 31.45 mW rail sum is correct, but the AP63205 supplies the 7 mA 3.3 V load through a 5 V LDO: its output is therefore approximately `5 V × (7 + 1.67) mA = 43.4 mW`, not 31 mW. The [DS41326 efficiency curve](https://www.diodes.com/assets/Datasheets/AP63200-AP63201-AP63203-AP63205.pdf) is about 88% for 12 V→5 V at this roughly 8.7 mA output load; 80% is conservative. Expected battery input is approximately 49 mW at 88%, or 54 mW at 80%. Thus 40 mW is optimistic by roughly 20–35%, not by 2×. [POWER.md:50](POWER.md:50)

2. **(a) arithmetic error — the 100%-duty panel row rounds below its own 2.5× rule.** Recomputing exactly with `H=0.96` and system factor 0.70:

   | duty | E (Wh/d) | break-even Wp | ×2.5 Wp | battery Ah |
   |---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | 0% | 3.84 | 5.71 | 14.29 | 6.40 |
   | 25% | 10.01 | 14.90 | 37.24 | 16.68 |
   | 50% | 16.18 | 24.08 | 60.19 | 26.97 |
   | 100% | 28.53 | 42.46 | **106.14** | 47.55 |

   The 15/40/60 W and 7/20/30/50 Ah rounded rows are otherwise coherent. A 100 W panel provides about 2.36×, not 2.5×, at 100% duty. [POWER.md:114](../docs/POWER.md:114)

3. **(b) unstated assumption that happens to hold — 0.70 and 2.5 are coherent only under a particular interpretation.** `H(i)_m` already accounts for the 70° plane, so 0.70 must be described as an end-to-end harvest/charging/wiring factor, not an “MPPT/tilt” derate. It is plausibly conservative. With a 2.5× panel, the nominal surplus is `1.5E/day`, so a seven-day `7E` deficit recharges in about **4.7 design-average clear days**. That implicit recovery target makes 2.5 coherent. Worst-of-19 weather plus a recovery/snow reserve is not intrinsically double-counting, but snow, soiling and recharge cannot each consume the full same margin simultaneously. [POWER.md:114](../docs/POWER.md:114)

4. **(c) real design defect — monthly PVGIS totals do not establish uninterrupted winter operation.** The PVGIS field, December minimum and mean are correctly read, but `H(i)_m` contains no daily sequence or snow-cover outage. Therefore “carries … through the worst December” is stronger than the analysis: a month can have adequate total energy while containing a battery-exhausting dark or snow-covered run. A daily simulation or explicit outage scenario is required for that claim. [POWER.md:94](POWER.md:94), [POWER.md:127](POWER.md:127)

5. **(c) real design defect — VE.Direct cannot retire the load estimate with the proposed wiring.** The load is explicitly wired directly to the battery, bypassing the controller’s load output. The [SmartSolar datasheet](https://www.victronenergy.com/upload/documents/Datasheet-SmartSolar-charge-controller-MPPT-75-10,-75-15,-100-15,-100-20_48V-EN.pdf) reports controller/battery charging current and load-output current; it cannot sense an external battery-connected probe current. Measuring that load requires the controller load output or a shunt/appropriate battery monitor. [POWER.md:153](POWER.md:153), [POWER.md:160](POWER.md:160)

6. **(c) real design defect — 100 W is not the 75/10’s panel ceiling.** Victron specifies 10 A charge current, **13 A maximum PV short-circuit current**, 145 W nominal PV power at 12 V, and explicitly says excess connected PV power is limited. Dividing panel watts by 12.8 V does not establish an unsafe PV input. A 150 W module may clip slightly but is not disqualified unless its cold Voc exceeds 75 V or Isc exceeds 13 A. Separately, `28 × [1 + 0.003×45] = 31.78 V`, not 31 V, although the safety conclusion remains ample. [POWER.md:251](POWER.md:251)

7. **(c) real design defect — the selected 25 Ah battery fails the document’s seven-day/cold/50%-DoD requirement.** The formula requires 26.97 Ah, correctly rounded to 30 Ah in the sizing table, but the buy list selects 25 Ah. Its allowed cold energy is `25×12×0.5×0.7 = 105 Wh`, versus 113.3 Wh required: about **6.5 days**, before ageing. The cycle-life justification is also sourced incorrectly: the current [Victron Super Cycle datasheet](https://www.victronenergy.com/upload/documents/Datasheet-AGM-Super-Cycle-battery-EN.pdf) states at least 300 cycles at 100% DoD, 700 at 60%, and 1000 at 40%; it does not state 300 at 80% or 2500 at 20%. Twenty percent nominal DoD is reached after 3.7 no-sun days, not “after a week”; seven days is 37.8% nominal or about 54% of the derated cold capacity. [POWER.md:120](POWER.md:120), [POWER.md:202](POWER.md:202), [POWER.md:233](POWER.md:233)

8. **(c) real design defect — the exact LIONTRON specification has been miscopied.** The official [LI1220LX datasheet](https://liontron.com/download/english/LI1220LX_EN.pdf) says charge **0…+45°C**, discharge **−20…+60°C**, and **IP65**. The document gives −5…+65°C, −25…+65°C and IP20, and generalizes IP20 to a possible LiFePO₄ purchase. Model, EAN 4260586370249, 256 Wh, ≥3000 cycles at 90% DoD, 2.9 kg and seven-year warranty do match. The corrected 0°C floor strengthens rather than weakens the AGM recommendation. [POWER.md:193](POWER.md:193), [POWER.md:245](POWER.md:245), [POWER.md:265](POWER.md:265)

9. **(c) real design defect — the BMS compatibility inference is stronger than its source.** The named JBD, JK, Daly, Seplos, Renogy, Redodo, Ective and Offgridtec families do appear in the current [BMS_BLE-HA supported list](https://github.com/patman15/BMS_BLE-HA). That does not support “any smart LiFePO₄ in this class is very likely readable”; discovery can depend on name patterns and exact firmware/protocol. Compatibility should be checked by exact pack model before purchase. Victron Battery Sense is separately supported by the official [Home Assistant integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/victron_ble/). [POWER.md:173](POWER.md:173)

10. **(a) arithmetic error — 0.2 V is the full supply loop drop, not the ground-line drop.** At 20°C, 30 m of 0.5 mm² copper is about 1.05 Ω per conductor, hence roughly **0.096 V** on GND at 91 mA; the two-conductor loop is about 0.19 V. At 72°C those figures rise to approximately 0.115 V and 0.23 V. “Three orders below” the −7…+12 V range is also false and compares unlike quantities. [CABLE-SOURCING.md:31](CABLE-SOURCING.md:31), [CABLE-SOURCING.md:39](CABLE-SOURCING.md:39)

11. **(c) real design defect — the THVD1410 common-mode range does not prove heater-PWM immunity.** The [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/thvd1410.pdf) specifies operation over ±15 V common mode; that is not a crosstalk-rejection specification. Unequal mutual capacitance/inductance to A and B, layer-stranding, connector breakout and shared impedance can convert the heater edge into differential voltage, while receiver thresholds are only tens to hundreds of millivolts. Truebner’s unshielded 300–400 m bus has no equivalent PWM aggressor. An overall shield also does little against coupling originating inside the same jacket. Require a defined twisted A/B pair or qualify the exact cable with worst-case PWM and scope/error testing. [CABLE-SOURCING.md:31](CABLE-SOURCING.md:31)

12. **(c) real design defect — the 8 K thermal margin is an environmental assumption, not qualification.** The cable is technically within its +80°C fixed-installation rating at the measured 72.1°C. Current derating is immaterial: even at 72°C, 91 mA dissipates only about **21 mW over the entire 30 m supply loop**, so self-heating is negligible. But a jacket temperature rating is an operating limit for the specified installation condition, not evidence of multi-year compost life at 72°C. The recommendation assumes the single measured peak bounds future seasons, depth gradients and sensor error. That is too weak for buried procurement unless the risk is explicitly accepted or the 125°C option is used. [CABLE-SOURCING.md:6](CABLE-SOURCING.md:6), [CABLE-SOURCING.md:135](CABLE-SOURCING.md:135), [POTTING.md:382](POTTING.md:382)

13. **(c) real design defect — LAPP’s words are quoted faithfully, but “qualified” is too strong.** The 2017 [LAPP product-information sheet](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/2547203.pdf) names composting plants and says “good resistance” to ammonia compounds and biogases. It gives no concentration, immersion duration, temperature, test method or service-life qualification; the [current LAPP page](https://www.lapp.com/de/de/oelflex-robust-215-c/p/0022704) omits those application claims. “LAPP qualified this exact cable for this exact application” and “qualification no general-purpose cable carries” overstate vendor application guidance. [CABLE-SOURCING.md:78](CABLE-SOURCING.md:78), [CABLE-SOURCING.md:83](CABLE-SOURCING.md:83), [POTTING.md:376](POTTING.md:376)

14. **(c) real design defect — S11’s disclosure understates a part/manufacturer mismatch.** The proposed [eBay cable](https://www.ebay.de/itm/274834113899) is Lumonic/IT-Tronics, whereas S11 is a LAPP-family source. Moreover, LAPP article **1030240 is 5×0.34 mm²**; LAPP’s 4×0.34 article is 1030239. The current [LAPP LiYY BK data](https://products.lappgroup.com/online-catalogue/data-communication-systems/low-frequency-data-transmission-cables/din-colour-code/unitronic-liyy-bk.html?type=1664268841) also describes UV/outdoor suitability and some oil resistance, contradicting “not UV-stabilised/not outdoor” and “no stated oil resistance.” Rejecting the exact Lumonic cable for lack of verified burial, compost chemistry and temperature data remains prudent, but those alleged independent disqualifiers were not established. [CABLE-SOURCING.md:45](CABLE-SOURCING.md:45), [CABLE-SOURCING.md:270](CABLE-SOURCING.md:270)

15. **(c) real design defect — the buried-splice permission is an inference presented as vendor approval.** Truebner’s [FAQ](https://www.truebner.de/de/faq_sensor.php) says the extension contact point must never be in soil. Its [extension guide](https://www.truebner.de/de/anleitung_kabelverlaengerung.php) requires the outer adhesive sleeve itself to be suitable for soil, but does not explicitly withdraw the FAQ prohibition. The proposed reconciliation may be intended, but the source does not establish it. Keep the joint above grade/accessible unless Truebner confirms buried use in writing. The CellPack identity, X774691, EAN and €8.21 price themselves checked out on the [live product page](https://www.voelkner.de/products/1026046/). [CABLE-SOURCING.md:168](CABLE-SOURCING.md:168), [CABLE-SOURCING.md:205](CABLE-SOURCING.md:205), [POTTING.md:405](POTTING.md:405)

16. **(c) real design defect — abrasion alone does not establish potting adhesion.** “PUR and TPE are both jackets a PU pot will grip once abraded” is formulation-dependent and has no supporting adhesion or peel test. Treat this as unqualified until the prescribed terminated-cable coupon demonstrates adhesion after thermal/wet exposure. [POTTING.md:393](POTTING.md:393)

## SURVIVED

- Heater arithmetic: `12²/140 = 1.0286 W`, or 85.7 mA.
- AP63205’s 80% light-load efficiency assumption is conservative at the actual converter load; the defect is the rail-topology calculation.
- PVGIS uses the correct `H(i)_m` field, fixed 70° plane, azimuth 0°, horizon default and 19-year series; the quoted December minimum and mean are correct.
- A 0.70 end-to-end PV harvest factor is defensible as a conservative assumption, provided it is not presented as a 70° tilt correction.
- Victron 75/10 self-consumption is 10 mA load-off and 19 mA load-on; 10 mA at 12 V is 2.88 Wh/day and exceeds the probe board estimate.
- The panel’s 28 V Voc and 4.4 A Isc are safely inside the controller’s 75 V and 13 A PV limits; 145 W nominal PV power at 12 V is correct.
- Direct 4S NMC rejection is sound: standard full charge is `4×4.2 = 16.8 V`, above the verified 15 V probe ceiling. A BMS normally protects limits; it does not make an ordinary 16.8 V pack a ≤15 V source.
- Truebner’s TrueFlex microbe-resistant, waterproof and direct-burial claims are faithful to its current FAQ; the absence of a published cable temperature rating is correctly disclosed.
- LAPP article numbers 0022704 and 1024482, conductor counts, ODs, temperature ranges and numbered-black-core metadata check out.
- The main solar basket’s roughly €247 gross total, LIONTRON’s listed current prices, Letronic’s roughly €175 net 30 m subtotal before freight, and the CellPack part metadata check out.
