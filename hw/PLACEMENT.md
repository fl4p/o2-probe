# Rev-A final PCB placement

`o2-probe.kicad_pcb` is the only fabrication placement. Fabricate only from
the ZIP generated by `make_fab.py`.

Two horizontal Edge.Cuts slots cross the board at local y=84.0, immediately
gasward of the potting datum. Each is 5.5 × 1.5 mm, leaving 1.0 mm edge webs
and a 7.0 mm centre neck. A full-width y=83.0…85.0 rule area blocks zone fill
on F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu, and B.Cu, so planes cannot bypass the routed slots.
Only the explicitly audited routes and one narrow GND bridge cross it. A solid
B.Cu `/HEATER_LOW` zone spreads heat across the gas-side island from y=85.0 to
the ring end. These features reduce axial conduction toward the potted body;
they do not isolate a second temperature sensor. The previous 119 mm/two-TMP117 tip was replaced, shortening the
nominal cell-centre-to-board-end distance to 13.5 mm. The actual
O₂-cell-to-PTFE-membrane spacing is set by the enclosure, the soldered ring,
and the membrane carrier; verify it on the 1:1 tube coupon.

## Functional placement

- Cable end through pot line: cable lands, protection and power, MCU, RS-485,
  programming, expansion, guarded AFE, and ADC.
- U6 occupies the right-side lane beside U5. Its 180° rotation faces the
  RS-485 interface toward U5 while keeping SWD and downstream low-speed buses
  out of the transceiver/cable lane. C8 is at `(18,37.5)` to clear U6 while
  remaining the local 3V3A-LDO output bypass.
- Unpotted chamber: SGX O₂ cell, SHT45, two heater resistors, heater switch,
  and one TMP117 measuring the warm tip near the membrane carrier.
- The manual-solder ring contacts are 2.0 × 3.0 mm PTH pads with 1.0 × 2.0 mm
  plated slots bisected by the left/right profile. They are explicitly marked
  castellated and joined in PCB copper;
  electrical continuity does not depend on the removable metal ring.
- U14/C23/C24 remain a complete DNP BMP390 option on the warm tip.

## Provisional physical gates

- Fit the real SGX part and verify its rear aperture before production.
- Provide the ring drawing/material/coating, confirm its dry-side insulation,
  membrane clamping, solder access, and thermal contact before ordering volume.
- The ring is on switched `HEATER_LOW`, not ground. It must not contact the wet
  gas path, enclosure metal, or accessible conductive parts.
- Verify the shortened end, PTFE spacing, unpotted volume, potting flash,
  carrier, and connector sweep in the actual 32 mm tube coupon.
- Independently verify the DNP BMP390 land pattern before fitting U14.
