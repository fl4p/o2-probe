# O2 probe fabrication-readiness gate

Status date: 2026-08-23

## Decision

**Ready for a bare-board first article at a capability-matched fabricator.**
Before ordering, the chosen fabricator must explicitly accept the supplied
0.09/0.15 mm trace/space floor, 0.25/0.15 mm via/drill, 1.00 mm four-layer
stack, and the intentional 0.80 mm routed slots. This is not an automated-PCBA
or production-qualified release. A physical SGX cell fit, enclosure coupon fit,
and the electrical/environmental bench programme remain mandatory.

The orderable artifact is generated by `make_fab.py` at
`fab/o2-probe-rev-a-fab.zip`. The script runs ERC, schematic connectivity,
error and warning DRC, schematic parity, the calibrated PCB audit, and checks
the Gerbers and Excellon files that it places in the ZIP.

## Closed fabrication blockers

- KiCad ERC: 0 errors and 0 warnings; schematic audit: PASS, 109 components and
  93 nets.
- KiCad PCB: 0 DRC errors, 0 unconnected items, and 0 schematic-parity issues.
- All 110 embedded-footprint/library mismatches were removed by resynchronising
  placed footprints from their authoritative libraries.
- CELL_RE is one 9.652 mm, 0.15 mm F.Cu segment from U7.13 to U11.2, with no
  via and no test point. U7 is wholly on the potted side of the dam.
- The low-impedance `GUARD_REPLICA` net now drives the F.Cu enclosure around
  the long CELL_RE run.
- The In2 GND plane is split across both the 3.5 mm potting transition and the
  U13 thermal barrier. Each region remains filled and rejoins through one
  deliberate 0.25 mm bridge, avoiding a full-width copper heat strap to U13.
- Two 7.5 × 0.8 mm transverse slots at local `y=114.0` interrupt the
  heater-to-U13 path. Their 0.75 mm edge webs and 3.5 mm centre neck reduce the
  direct FR-4 cross-section from 20.0 to 5.0 mm.
- TP7 and every `/TMP_GAS_EP` segment stay gasward of that barrier, so the
  exposed thermal pad does not create a direct copper strap to the heater side.
- TP7 is lateral to U13 at local `(15,116)`, the DNP BMP390/C23/C24 option is
  heaterward of the barrier, and the rounded gas end is 6 mm shorter. Fitted
  C22 remains beside U13.
- The audit locks the complete cross-barrier bundle: one each of 3V3D, SCL,
  SDA, GND, and TGAS_ALERT. U12 joins SDA heaterward of the barrier instead of
  adding a redundant second crossing.
- The SGX footprint now follows DS-0637 Issue 2 for the Ø14.40 mm body, Ø0.64
  mm pins, 2.54 mm pitch, and 2.50 mm row offset. A gasward-offset 3 × 6 mm
  NPTH slot provides a first-article airflow/fit window without assuming an
  undocumented circular rear-port diameter.
- Board rules and the nominal stackup are explicit: four copper layers, 1.00
  mm finished thickness, 0.09 mm minimum track, 0.15 mm minimum clearance,
  0.25/0.15 mm minimum via/drill, 1 oz outer copper, 0.5 oz inner copper, and
  ENIG finish.
- `audit_pcb.py --calibrate` rejects 17/17 deliberately broken variants,
  including a CELL_RE via, missing guard, unsplit ground regression, 0.08 mm
  track, moved U7, missing/unfilled GND regions, removed/deformed U13 barrier,
  missing U13 GND neck, heater-side U13 or TP7, track and non-track copper heat
  bypasses, a deformed non-thermal slot, a deformed outer corner, a notched
  controlled GND region, and missing stackup. The release builder separately
  validates the exact R2 outer contour and all six contours in the Edge.Cuts
  Gerber.

## Reviewed warnings and release scope

KiCad reports 28 courtyard-overlap warnings. These are dense-placement/manual-
assembly review items, not bare-board manufacturability errors. The exact pair
allowlist is locked in `audit_pcb.py` and recorded in
`ASSEMBLY-EXCEPTIONS.md`; any added or removed pair fails the release audit.

Physical legend Gerbers are deliberately absent from the fab ZIP. The remaining
38 silkscreen warnings therefore do not describe a printed manufacturing layer.
Mask, copper, holes, routing, outline, and connectivity have no warning waiver.
The source legend is present in the included multipage fabrication/assembly
drawing: its `POT TO HERE` and `DO NOT OBSTRUCT` marks are controlled traveller
and fixture instructions for this no-legend first article. Physical legend must
be cleaned and released before production.

## First-article acceptance gates

These checks do not prevent ordering the first bare boards; they do prevent
production or automated assembly release:

- Fit a real SGX-OX-ROHS-Mini from the intended lot. Gauge pins, verify PCB-side
  CE/RE/WE identity, confirm body standoff, and inspect that the 3 × 6 mm slot
  leaves both gas faces unobstructed.
- Run a 1:1 board coupon in the actual 32 mm PVC-U tube, carrier, potting dam,
  and membrane stack. Verify connector chord, component sweep, potting flash,
  slot endpoints, the shortened gas end, membrane clearance, and the unpotted
  gas volume.
- Obtain written or portal confirmation from the selected bare-board vendor
  for every capability listed in the decision above. No vendor is inferred
  from this repository.
- Hand-assemble the first article and inspect every courtyard pair listed in
  `ASSEMBLY-EXCEPTIONS.md`. Do not submit this revision for turnkey automated
  PCBA from the bare-board ZIP.
- Complete VREF startup/noise tests, heater thermal and stuck-on tests, TMP117
  calibration, N2/air/30% O2 calibration, condensation/soak tests, and potting
  process coupons.
- Freeze a procurement BOM with exact passive ratings and manufacturer part
  numbers before any production PCBA order. The schematic BOM remains a design
  BOM, not a purchasing release.
