## Findings and repairs

- **C36 ground via overlapped the capacitor land.** Via `(116.45,108.6)` overlapped C36.2 copper by approximately 0.01 mm. Private repair moves it to `(116.9,108.6)` and adjusts its two attached GND segments. No new via is added.
- Two other REF-cluster GND vias had small positive land gaps: approximately 0.0654 mm to U15.1 and 0.1309 mm to U15.6. These are **placement improvements, not violations of a declared universal 0.20 mm rule**. The same private adapter moves them to `(117.15,112.9)` and `(113.175,112.1)`. The three repaired vias have nearest native SMD copper gaps of at least 0.4399, 0.3950 and 0.3249 mm, respectively.
- **C3 ground via also overlaps its land.** Via `(116.4,73.525)` overlaps C3.2 because the rotated capacitor pad extends 0.9 mm horizontally from its centre. The separate upper adapter moves it to `(116.725,73.525)` and adjusts its two ground track endpoints.

Exact repair: `checks/power-independent-review/ref_via_repair_adapter.py`. It changes three existing vias and six attached track endpoints, locks all nine changed objects, preserves force/sense wiring and adds no copper objects. Full native DRC (`--refill-zones --save-board --all-track-errors --severity-all`) of the private repaired board reports no errors involving that delta and no GND opens. Its older bus crossings and intentionally unfinished power nets remain; the root owns their replacement. Evidence: `checks/power-independent-review/repair/drc.json` and `receipt.json`.

The requested whole-board diagnostic checked all 169 vias against all SMD copper shapes on both sides. A 0.20 mm exploratory margin finds 45 pairs involving 40 vias; this is **not a process gate**. At zero margin, nine copper overlap/touch pairs remain: U7.15, C20.2, C3.2, C21.2, R29.2, Q2.2, R44.2, U16.3 and Q2.1. All except U7.15 have mask and paste on the matched pad; U7.15 is the exposed pad with separate paste apertures. Exact geometry is in `repair/overlap-only.json`. Copper overlap alone does not establish whether the drilled hole enters the paste aperture; these cases need that separate classification before final assembly acceptance. The user’s 0.10 mm minimum copper clearance remains the electrical floor.

The upper follow-up also found that U7’s EP via at `(109.1,113.15)` sits within a separate paste window centred at `(109.35,112.85)`, despite the numbered EP pad having no paste layer itself. Removing the via broke ground connectivity, so `checks/power-independent-review/upper_overlap_adapter.py` relocates this existing via to `(108.475,114.1)` and adds a 0.425 mm, 0.15 mm-wide F.Cu ground stub into the EP. Its copper is outside the solder land; the small positive same-net gap is not misreported as a 0.20 mm process violation. No additional via is used.

Combined validation includes the finished bus geometry and `checks/mcu-analog/vin_bus_clearance_adapter.py`, which moves the VIN_MON transition clear of SDA. `repair/combined-drc.json` reports **zero electrical/geometric errors**, zero GND or bus opens, and 17 remaining unconnected pairs confined to the deliberately unfinished `/3V3D`, `/+12V_SW` and `/HEATER_PWM` nets. The private-directory footprint-library configuration errors and existing warnings are not claimed cleared. Remaining overlap cases belong to the root/lower-board repair; the final integrated board still needs its own checks.

## Local engineering assessment

The buck input switching loop is entirely on F.Cu: C1.1 to U1 VIN is approximately 2.57 mm of 0.40 mm copper, and U1 GND to C1.2 approximately 2.41 mm. The 100 nF bootstrap capacitor connects BST to SW with no layer transition; its BST path is approximately 1.68 mm. The main SW connection to L1 is approximately 2.11 mm, also on F.Cu. L1 output to populated C3 uses 0.50 mm copper. This preserves the local input loop and avoids a bootstrap via detour. The separate upper adapter corrects the C3 ground-via overlap.

The current netlist uses AP63203, 4.7 µH L1, 10 µF input, 100 nF bootstrap and one populated 22 µF output capacitor; C4 is DNP. Diodes’ example specifies 3.9 µH and two 22 µF output capacitors, while its general guidance permits 2.2–10 µH and 22–68 µF ceramic output capacitance. This supports the local arrangement but does not verify effective capacitance under bias or transient behaviour. C3’s existing design note already requires effective capacitance above 9.1 µF before release; that qualification remains open.

REF3425 pin mapping matches the current netlist: 1 GND_F, 2 GND_S, 3 EN, 4 IN, 5 OUT_S, 6 OUT_F. GND_S joins GND_F locally; OUT_S joins OUT_F locally. C10 returns between OUT_F and GND_F through short same-face tracks. The 4.7 µF output capacitor and 100 nF local input bypass are within TI’s application guidance. The local force/sense ties are permissible when local IR drop is negligible; they do not provide remote Kelvin correction at the MCU. EN and IN share `/3V3D` in the netlist; completion of the withdrawn EN/distribution copper is still required.
