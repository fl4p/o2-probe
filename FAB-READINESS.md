# O2 probe fabrication-readiness gate

Status date: 2026-08-24

## Decision

**Ready for a bare-board first article at a capability-matched fabricator.**
**Not ready to source parts from: `audit_bom.py` reports FAIL.** Bare boards are
unaffected, but 16 fitted components still carry a value family instead of an
orderable MPN, and the Rev A purchase orders are already three parts short
because of it. See `bom-audit.rpt` before building any basket.
The selected process must accept 0.09/0.15 mm trace/space, 0.25/0.15 mm
via/drill, 0.20 mm copper-to-routed-edge clearance, two plated-slot edge
castellations, all four internal thermal slots, and
a 1.00 mm four-layer stack. This is not an automated-PCBA,
heated-ring assembly, or production-qualified release. Real SGX fit, enclosure
fit, ring mechanics, and environmental qualification remain mandatory.

The orderable artifact is generated by `make_fab.py` at
`fab/o2-probe-rev-a-fab.zip`. The builder runs ERC, connectivity audits, error
and warning DRC, schematic parity, known-bad calibration, exact Gerber/Excellon
validation, and the procurement audit. The last one does not gate bare boards,
but its verdict is stamped into `fab-manifest.json` under `bom` and into the
manifest's own `qualification_scope`, so a release can no longer describe itself
as ready while its parts are unbuyable.

## Closed fabrication blockers

- KiCad ERC has 0 violations; the schematic audit passes with 106 components,
  336 assigned pins, and 92 nets.
- KiCad PCB DRC has 0 errors and 0 unconnected items. The release does not
  rely on KiCad 10.0.5's parity result: `audit_pcb.py` independently compares
  the exact schematic XML reference set and every numbered PCB pad net, with
  zero mismatches on the release.
- CELL_RE remains one 9.652 mm, 0.15 mm F.Cu segment from U7.13 to U11.2,
  without a via or test point. Four exact stitch vias and ten short In1/B.Cu
  links retain three physically connected `GUARD_REPLICA` fill regions on both
  sides of the long run; the audit checks the saved filled polygons, not only
  the zone source outline.
- The board is exactly 20.00 × 107.00 × 1.00 mm with four copper layers, an
  exact six-primitive outline with R2 cable-end corners and a square gas end,
  and four exact thermal-slot contours. The two 6.75 × 1.50 mm horizontal
  slots are centred at `(104.125,134.00)` and `(115.875,134.00)` mm and retain
  0.75 mm edge webs plus a 5.00 mm centre neck. The two 0.80 × 16.00 mm
  vertical slots are centred at `(101.20,145.00)` and `(118.80,145.00)` mm,
  span y=137.00–153.00 mm, and have no track, via, or pad crossing.
- U11's separate 3.00 × 6.00 mm gasward NPTH oblong is centred at absolute
  `(110.00,145.30)` mm, 0.50 mm from pad-2 copper. The release validates its
  tool, exact route endpoints, pad gap, and the rerouted inner-layer heater
  feed independently.
- The obsolete U13/C22/R30/TP7 second-temperature circuit and both of its
  dedicated nets are absent from the schematic, PCB, and release.
- J5 is exactly two 2.00 × 3.00 mm through-hole corner pads at the left/right
  gas-side edges. Their 1.00 × 2.00 mm plated slots carry KiCad's castellated
  fabrication property and are bisected by the finished profile. PCB copper
  connects both contacts; the metal ring is not an electrical jumper.
- `HEATER_LOW` uses only ≥0.80 mm B.Cu tracks in the tip and a solid B.Cu
  heater-spreader zone covers the gas-side island. U10 sits 2.975 mm from the
  ring centre; R29 provides passive default-off, and the wired TMP117 ALERT
  path is not credited as an overtemperature cutoff until configured and
  fault-tested in firmware.
- The first-order y=133–135 mm conduction network gives about 98 K/W from the
  gas-side island to the body, with a 90–110 K/W engineering bound. The
  vertical side slots reduce lateral edge-rail coupling but do not add their
  full 16 mm length in series because a 2.25 mm unslotted connection remains
  between them and the transverse slots. `THERMAL-BARRIER.md` records the
  geometry, all 14 track/two-via paths, assumptions, limits, and measurement
  method; this estimate is not a production qualification result.
- The thermal-barrier copper inventory is locked: 14 track segments, two
  vias, no footprint pads, copper graphics, or footprint zones, the exact F.Cu
  `GUARD_REPLICA` source zone plus its three actual filled regions, a named
  full-width all-layer pour gap from y=133…135 mm, the B.Cu `/HEATER_LOW`
  spreader, and one In2 GND source zone saved as two filled regions. One exact
  0.15 mm three-segment In2 bridge preserves GND continuity across the gap. Fitted
  U10/U12 use dogbone ground vias rather than via-in-pad.
- Board rules and nominal stackup explicitly record the advanced-fab floor,
  1 oz outer/0.5 oz inner copper, and ENIG.
- U6 is locked to the dense right-side lane at local `(14.5,44.5)`, 180°,
  beside U5; C8 is locked to `(18,37.5)` so the compact cluster has no new
  component-courtyard overlap.
- The generator assigns `RS485_DE` to PC6 beside the USART1 TX/RX pins and
  `VA_MON` to PA7/ADC_IN7; PA0/PA1 are intentionally unused. Firmware must use
  this final pin map.
- `audit_pcb.py --calibrate` rejects 33/33 deliberately broken variants,
  including a CELL_RE via, missing guard/GND plane, unexpected third slot,
  deformed outline/thermal-slot/GND/guard/ring geometry, disconnected actual
  guard fill, unfilled plane, an unplated ring contact, stale second-sensor
  copper, unexpected barrier track/graphic copper, narrow
  heater/ordinary/switched-supply tracks,
  moved U7/U6, exact duplicate copper, deleted schematic footprint, wrong pad
  net, missing stackup, disabled thermal pour gap, wrong heater-spreader net,
  and a missing narrow GND bridge.
- `make_fab.py` independently finds all four exact thermal-slot contours in the
  Edge.Cuts Gerber, both exact J5 plated-slot castellations in PTH Excellon,
  the one exact SGX routed NPTH in Excellon, and all three
  driven-guard regions plus eight interior coverage samples in the F.Cu Gerber.
  It also verifies seven pour-free samples across every copper-layer Gerber,
  the B.Cu heater spreader, and both In2 GND regions.

## Reviewed warnings and scope

KiCad reports 27 courtyard overlaps. Their exact pair set is locked in
`audit_pcb.py` and listed in `ASSEMBLY-EXCEPTIONS.md`; they are hand-assembly
review items, not bare-board errors. The remaining 33 legend warnings are 18
silk overlaps, 14 silk-over-copper findings, and one silk-to-edge finding on
layers intentionally omitted from the Gerber release. There are no waivers for
copper, mask, holes, outline, connectivity, or schematic parity.

## First-article acceptance gates

These do not block ordering bare boards, but they do block production:

- Fit a real SGX-OX-ROHS-Mini, gauge its pins, verify PCB-side CE/RE/WE, body
  standoff, and unobstructed gas faces around the 3 × 6 mm aperture.
- Freeze the ring drawing, alloy/plating, thickness, joint geometry, membrane
  clamping method, and electrical insulation. J5 is switched `HEATER_LOW`, not
  ground; no ring metal may reach the wet face, enclosure, or accessible parts.
- Coupon the 20 × 107 mm board, ring, PTFE membrane, carrier, potting dam, and
  connector in the actual 32 mm tube. Measure the final O₂-to-membrane spacing
  rather than inferring it from the PCB outline.
- Confirm the selected fabricator plates and profiles the two J5 castellations
  as documented; inspect sidewall continuity and copper adhesion on coupons.
- Hand assemble and inspect the reviewed courtyard pairs and the J5 solder
  operation.
  Do not submit the bare-board ZIP for turnkey automated PCBA.
- Run heater open/short/stuck-on protection tests and map ring, membrane, U10,
  U12, SGX body, and ambient temperatures at 0/intermediate/100% drive.
- Measure temperatures immediately on both sides of the transverse barrier
  during at least three steady heater-power steps. Treat
  `delta_T / P_electrical` only as a lower bound; qualify the PCB value with
  body-side heat-flow measurement or a guarded loss correction as specified
  in `THERMAL-BARRIER.md`.
- Run controlled dew-point crossings and humid soak tests. Demonstrate that
  the membrane stays above local dew point without overheating or biasing the
  electrochemical cell; validate the condensation model against measurements.
- Complete TMP117 calibration, VREF/noise tests, N₂/air/30% O₂ calibration,
  and potting coupons.
- Clear `audit_bom.py` before ordering parts, not merely before production
  PCBA. This gate previously read "freeze exact procurement MPNs before
  production PCBA", which left hand-built first-article purchasing uncovered:
  L1, DZ1 and F1 were bought by nobody because nothing checked. The audit
  resolves every fitted component to an exact MPN or an explicitly declared
  class in `sourcing.json`, and diffs the result against
  `purchase-orders.json`.
